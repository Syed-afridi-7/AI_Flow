# Landing Page Converter

## Overview
Transforms product features and business requirements into high-converting, direct-response landing pages. Synthesizes value propositions, visitor psychographics, objection handling, social proof architecture, and modern responsive frontend wireframes into a unified conversion engine.

## When to Use
- **Trigger symptoms:** When launching a new product or feature, rebuilding an underperforming marketing page, designing marketing funnels, preparing for Product Hunt or ad campaigns, or seeking to improve visitor-to-signup conversion rates.
- **When NOT to use:** Internal admin dashboards, behind-the-login application views, pure API documentation, or complex multi-step checkout checkout carts.

## Instructions

Execute the conversion architecture through four standardized phases:

### Phase 1: Core Value Proposition & Psychographic Positioning
1. **Target Persona & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** Identify the primary decision-maker, their burning operational pain points, and existing flawed workarounds.
2. **Value Proposition Formula:** Formulate the primary headline following the high-conversion formula: `[Action Verb] + [Desired Outcome] + [Timeframe/Mechanism] + [Without Pain Point]`.
3. **Sub-headline & Anxiety Reducers:** Craft a 2-sentence explanatory subhead detailing how the product delivers on the headline promise, paired with immediate micro-trust signals ("No credit card required", "Free 14-day trial", "Setup in 2 minutes").

### Phase 2: Page Flow Architecture & Narrative Arc
Organize the landing page sections following the proven Direct-Response narrative arc:
1. **Hero Section (Above the Fold):** Eyebrow badge, H1 headline, subhead, primary CTA, secondary exploratory CTA, micro-trust indicators, and a product preview/demo screenshot.
2. **Social Proof Marquee:** Customer/client logos, featured media badges, or active user/transaction metrics.
3. **Problem & Agitation Breakdown:** Articulate 3 vivid, relatable problems the visitor is experiencing right now.
4. **Product Solution & Feature Benefits:** 3-4 feature spotlights pairing product capabilities with measurable business benefits (Feature &rarr; Advantage &rarr; Tangible Benefit).
5. **Interactive Demo / Product Walkthrough:** Visual step-by-step breakdown (1-2-3 simplicity).
6. **Quantified Case Studies & Testimonials:** High-credibility quotes including author name, avatar, job title, company, and concrete metrics achieved.
7. **Transparent Pricing Matrix:** 2-3 transparent pricing tiers with feature checklists, recommended badge, and billing toggles (Monthly/Annual).
8. **Objection-Busting FAQ:** 5-7 direct answers to primary purchase objections (security, migration effort, lock-in, support, integrations).
9. **Risk-Reversal Final CTA:** High-urgency closing section with a risk-free trial guarantee.

### Phase 3: Direct-Response Copywriting & CTA Engineering
1. **Action-Oriented CTAs:** Use first-person active verbs (e.g., "Start My Free 14-Day Trial", "Build Your First Token System") rather than passive labels ("Submit", "Click Here").
2. **Cognitive Fluency:** Maintain an 8th-grade reading level using short paragraphs (2-3 sentences), bold key phrases, bullet points, and high scannability.

### Phase 4: Responsive Wireframe & Component Code
1. Produce semantic HTML5 / Tailwind CSS markup for the Hero and core conversion components.
2. Ensure mobile-first responsiveness with large touch targets (&ge; 48px) and accessible contrast ratios.

## Rules & Constraints
- **Clear Over Clever:** Headlines must immediately convey what the product is and does; never sacrifice clarity for wordplay.
- **Above-The-Fold Clarity:** A first-time visitor must comprehend the product value proposition within 5 seconds of landing.
- **Single Primary Action:** Every section must guide the user toward the single primary conversion goal; avoid competing calls to action.
- **Evidence-Backed Claims:** Replace generic adjectives ("faster", "better") with quantified outcomes ("3.5x faster builds", "cuts review time by 40%").

## Common Mistakes & Anti-Patterns
| Anti-Pattern | Correction |
| :--- | :--- |
| Feature-dumping without benefits | Translate every feature into an outcome: "Automated backups" &rarr; "Never lose critical data". |
| Generic or fake testimonials | Include real attribution: full name, verified company, photo, and specific metric. |
| Weak or hidden CTA buttons | Place primary CTA in sticky navigation, above the fold, mid-page, and at the footer. |
| Wall of text on mobile screens | Use bite-sized cards, icons, and scannable accordion patterns for mobile devices. |
